# CMPM120-assignment2

SPACE DEFENDERS - a Space Invaders / Galaxian style gallery shooter (Phaser 3)

CONTROLS
  LEFT / RIGHT arrows or A / D   move the ship (left/right only)
  SPACE                          fire
  ENTER                          start game / play again after Game Over or Victory
  C (on title screen)            controls & credits screen
  ESC                            return to title after Game Over / Victory (or leave controls screen)

THEME AND SCORING
You are the last defender ship fighting waves of invading aliens.
  Grunt (white/red invader)  10 points. Sits in the swaying formation; some peel off and dive
                             along a curved path toward you. Killing a diving grunt = 2x points (20).
  Swooper (small alien)      30 points. Flies in as a wave ("pulse") along a long S-shaped spline path
                             and fires aimed shots.
  Tank (heavy alien)         50 points, takes 2 hits, fires a 3-shot spread, and usually drops a power-up.
  Boss (big warship)         1000 points, plus a 500 point clear bonus.
  Yellow orb power-up        +50 points and double shot for 10 seconds (dropped by enemies).
  Clearing a level gives a bonus of 100 points per remaining life.
You get hit, thats one life down


ART AND AUDIO 
  Art:   "Space Shooter Extension" (ships, laser and power-up orb sprites)
  Sound: "Sci-Fi Sounds" (lasers, explosions, impacts, force field)
